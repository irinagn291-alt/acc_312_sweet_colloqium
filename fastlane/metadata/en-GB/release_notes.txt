Version 1.0. First release of Sweet Colloqium, a private week habit app for one habit at a time.

• Onboarding, three pages, with "Skip" at the top right on every page
• Rings with "Close" for today, seven-day rings, "Mend target", "This week", "Day chain", and "Lost beat held" or "Lost beat spent"
• One lost beat per week to Mend a silent past scheduled day so a "Mended miss. Still counts."
• Dashboard with "Closed days", "X of Y" "closed of scheduled", and "Streak N"
• Lost beat with "Mend" on a silent past scheduled day
• Settings with "Habit", "Scheduled weekdays", "Save habit", "Contact Stackfreed", "Re-run onboarding", and "Reset all data"
• Data stays on this device. Light only. Portrait only on iPhone and iPad. No camera, photos, microphone, location, notifications, or tracking.
